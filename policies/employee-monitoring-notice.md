**Organization:** [Company Name]  
**Effective Date:** [Date]

---

## 1. Purpose of This Notice

This notice is provided in compliance with the **Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173)** and its Implementing Rules and Regulations.

The purpose of this notice is to inform you, as an employee, that certain company-owned workstations are monitored using the **AgentWatch Remote Workstation Monitoring System**.

---

## 2. What Is Monitored

Only the following information may be accessed during an authorized monitoring session:

| What May Be Monitored | Purpose |
|----------------------|---------|
| **Live screen view** of your company workstation | Quality assurance, supervision, troubleshooting |
| **Session metadata** (start/end time, duration, requester) | Audit trail and accountability |
| **Device status** (online/offline) | IT operations |

---

## 3. What Is NOT Monitored

The following are explicitly **prohibited** by this system's design and policy:

- ❌ Keylogging (recording keystrokes)
- ❌ Webcam activation or recording
- ❌ Microphone activation or recording
- ❌ Covert or hidden screenshots
- ❌ Personal device monitoring
- ❌ Screen recording storage (screen viewing is live only; recordings are NOT stored by default)
- ❌ Monitoring outside of active business sessions

---

## 4. Who Can Monitor Your Workstation

Only authorized personnel with specific permission may view your workstation screen:

- **Your direct Manager** — only if explicitly assigned by a system administrator
- **Your Team Leader** — only if explicitly assigned
- **System Administrators** — for security incident response only

IT Support staff do NOT automatically have screen-viewing permissions.

---

## 5. When Monitoring Occurs

Monitoring only occurs when:

1. An authorized manager specifically requests a session
2. The system verifies the authorization
3. The session is recorded in the audit trail
4. **Your workstation displays a visible notification that monitoring is active**

---

## 6. Monitoring Notification

When your workstation is being monitored, a **visible notification** will appear:

> 🔴 **"Remote monitoring is currently active."**

When monitoring ends:

> ✅ **"Remote monitoring has ended."**

Your workstation always displays:

> ℹ️ **"Your workstation is managed by [Company]. Authorized monitoring may be active."**

---

## 7. Data Retention

- Session metadata (who requested, when, duration) is retained for **[90 days]** by default.
- Audit logs are retained for **[365 days]**.
- Screen content is **not stored**.

---

## 8. Your Rights Under RA 10173

As a data subject under the Philippine Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

- **Access** records of when your workstation was monitored (via HR or your manager)
- **Be informed** of this monitoring policy (this notice)
- **Raise concerns** to the company Data Privacy Officer (DPO)
